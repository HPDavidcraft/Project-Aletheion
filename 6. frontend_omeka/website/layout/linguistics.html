    PRODUCTION READINESS

    What this demo does not connect

    The page exercises the data model locally; publication or research use requires validated identifiers, evidence, geometry, and server-side checks.

    Classification and scripts

    Validate Glottolog identifiers against an adopted release, resolve parent links and SKOS writing-system concepts, and maintain versioned authority mappings.

    Spatial and temporal evidence

    Replace schematic polygons with valid PostGIS geography, validate EDTF against normalized year bounds, and query zones plus archaeological sites through the API at a selected date.

    Evidence and rights

    Attach verified bibliography records and link-level citations, rights-cleared Media_Gallery assets, editorial provenance, and server-derived trust inputs before publication.
