      PRODUCTION READINESS

      What must be wired for real research use

      The page works as a client-side demonstration. It is not connected to the database, publishing workflow, or external research services.

      Canonical records & validation

      PostGIS chronicle/phase geometry, EDTF parsing and range consistency, event description/interpretation/analysis publication gate, server-side bibliography requirements, and append-only editorial audit.

      References, media & people

      Verified Zotero UUIDs and link-level citation metadata; rights-cleared IIIF galleries; SKOS roles; real person/event/witness associations; Glottolog codes and validated textual witness identifiers.

      Spatial, temporal & network services

      GeoServer/OGC climate and polity layers, true time-indexed polygon geometry, spatial and temporal queries, graph traversal, and server-derived ruler, diplomatic, and chart data.

      Source criticism & debate

      Text-witness persistence and IIIF annotation versions, verified supporting/opposing citations, moderated interpretation workflows, and consensus indicators derived from traceable evidence.
